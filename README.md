## Selected Projects

### [Vopěnka's Principle without Choice: Preservation under Symmetric Extensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06856)

Preprint (September 2026) with Wojciech Aleksander Wołoszyn. We used initial AI-led proofs, then rewrote and substantially extended them.
I led the orchestration of a 300,000-line Lean formalization over 5 days, repairing and auditing upstream papers and arguments along the way.

### [HeXO](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/HeXO)

Self-play MCTS engine, trained with custom GPU kernels written in Triton, plus a novel graph-search scheduling algorithm.

### [Bazaar_ML](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/Bazaar_ML) (private)

RLGym-style reinforcement learning environment for The Bazaar, a deckbuilding roguelike.
The goal is to model the game loop, cards, actions, combat mechanics, and evaluation hooks well enough to train and compare agents.

### [Erdosproblems](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/Erdosproblems)

Umbrella repository for Lean formalization work around Erdos problems.
Current work includes problem-specific formalization efforts for problems such as 1066 and 1197.

### [BaanSoliciteren](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/BaanSoliciteren)

Agentic job-application tracker built with Python, JSON files, and Gmail integration. Got me a job, so a dead project once it fulfilled its task!
It extracts vacancy data, scores roles, handles application emails, updates statuses, and archives stale applications.

### [Hadamard_668](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/Hadamard_668)

Research/search notes for the order-668 Hadamard matrix problem.
This is partial progress and failed routes: base-sequence attempts, cyclic/SDS searches, and route-ranking artifacts. Good partials but nowhere close to a solve.

### [Collection-of-Projects](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/Collection-of-Projects)

Older notebooks and data projects from university years, including immigration trend analysis, COVID scenario modeling, and smaller data tooling experiments.

## Math & AI

I received the Rising Mathematician Sponsorship of $10,000 from Harmonic and contribute to open-source formal mathematics.
My first major Lean milestone was a fully agentic solution and [Lean formalization of First Proof problem 9](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/firstproof) (3 days, 2,500 lines of Lean), later referenced in [Daniel Litt's article](https://www.daniellitt.com/blog/2026/2/20/mathematics-in-the-library-of-babel).

Since then:

- [Prill's problem](https://www.problemsilike.com/14) on Daniel Litt's problem list: solved in all genera with GPT-5.6.
- [New Foundations](https://github.com/Tomodovodoo/NF_neg_WPP): a 17,174-line Lean proof that NF refutes the weak partition principle.
- [PrimeNumberTheoremAnd](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3ATomodovodoo+is%3Amerged): merged Lean proofs built with Aristotle and AI agents.
- Accepted participant in the [Caltech Mathathon 2026](https://mathathonchallenge.com/).

I focus on handling AI systems and tackling their weak points. In math, Lean proofs, tests, adversarial review, and multi-agent adversarial prompting are strong tools right now, and I aim to make the most of them.
